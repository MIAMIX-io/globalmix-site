Behind The BarBeyond The Ordinary

Every bar has a story, and behind every great cocktail is usually a little more than one person's idea.

A look back at the places, people, and creative experiences that shaped my journey through hospitality, from Atlanta to Miami and New York.

Bringing together original recipes, contributions from former colleagues, personal stories, and a visual archive of menus and inspiration, the collection celebrates the creativity that happens when talented people come together behind a bar. Each recipe is credited to its creator.
