Bespoke Lodge & Aerodrome

A Highly Differentiated Proposition

We combine three high-value concepts rarely integrated in Argentina: Bespoke rural hospitality, premium experiences, and direct private flight access. This transforms the hotel into a destination in itself.

Designed for an exclusive segment, including international tourists, executives, premium sports fishermen, and private aircraft owners. The experience begins the moment you land.

  • 13.2 Hectares of private grounds with natural landscaping
  • Exclusive capacity of 22 guests (8 Double, 2 Triple Rooms)
  • Total privacy, personalized service, and a relaxed atmosphere
  • Hangar, VIP reception, and pilot lounge on site
Experiential Luxury

Curated Experiences

Every aspect of the lodge is crafted to generate an emotional connection with the rural landscape and Argentine traditions.

Private Aviation

An 800 x 20m private airstrip designed for light aviation and turboprops. Placed at a perfect distance to ensure safety and silence.

Bespoke Suites

Designed with an efficient central corridor and private exterior galleries. Acoustic separation and panoramic views.

Regional Gastronomy

A spectacular glass-enclosed dining room with an open kitchen. Enjoy premium meats, open fires, and regional cuisine.

Dark Sky Observatory

An immersive scientific and sensory journey beneath the stars. Professional telescopes, astrophotography, and guided observation.

Spa & Wellness

Disconnect and rejuvenate. Holistic treatments, thermal circuits, and nature-inspired therapies designed for ultimate relaxation.

Equestrian & Outdoors

Experience the Pampas. Polo lessons, guided horseback expeditions, Golden Dorado fishing, and exclusive hunting reserves.

Design

Immersive Architecture

The single-story layout perfectly integrates into the environment, avoiding aggressive visual impact while maximizing accessibility and operational efficiency. The chosen aesthetic blends stone, wood, black metal, and large glass panes.

  • The Central Fogón: The nocturnal heart of the hotel. It provides an authentic Argentine identity—ideal for folklore, wine tastings, and stargazing.
  • Multi-Purpose Room (SUM): A flexible space designed for yoga, wellness, corporate events, workshops, and exclusive private gatherings.
The Journey

The Arrival Experience

The aerial entrance completely transforms the guest's perception, making the journey a fundamental part of the adventure.

I

Takeoff

Depart from Buenos Aires, Rosario, Córdoba, Montevideo, or Asunción in a private aircraft.

II

Scenic Flight

Enjoy a panoramic journey over the fields, estuaries, the Paraná River, and the native forests of Entre Ríos.

III

Direct Landing

Touch down directly inside the establishment on our secure 800m private runway.

IV

Premium Reception

Be greeted by a safari-style vehicle, champagne welcome, and a personalized check-in by the central fire pit.
