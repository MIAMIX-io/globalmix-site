Boutique Lodge & Private Aerodrome

We combine three high-value concepts rarely integrated in Argentina: boutique rural hospitality, premium experiences, and direct private flight access. This transforms the hotel into a destination in itself.

A Highly Differentiated Proposition

Designed for an exclusive segment, including international tourists, executives, premium sports fishermen, and private aircraft owners. The experience begins the moment you land.

  • 13.2 Hectares of private grounds with natural landscaping
  • Exclusive capacity of 22 guests (8 Double, 2 Triple Rooms)
  • Total privacy, personalized service, and a relaxed atmosphere
  • Hangar, VIP reception, and pilot lounge on site

Experiential Luxury

Every aspect of the lodge is crafted to generate an emotional connection with the rural landscape and Argentine traditions.

Private Aviation

An 800 x 20m private airstrip designed for light aviation and turboprops (Cessna, Piper, Caravan). Placed at a perfect distance to ensure safety and silence for guests.

Boutique Suites

Designed with an efficient central corridor and private exterior galleries. Acoustic separation and panoramic views position the hotel as an exclusive, low-density retreat.

Regional Gastronomy

A spectacular glass-enclosed dining room with an open kitchen. Enjoy premium meats, open fires, and regional cuisine with permanent visual connection to the countryside.

Immersive Architecture

The single-story layout perfectly integrates into the environment, avoiding aggressive visual impact while maximizing accessibility and operational efficiency. The chosen aesthetic blends stone, wood, black metal, and large glass panes.

The Central Fire Pit (Fogón): The nocturnal heart of the hotel. It provides an authentic Argentine identity—ideal for folklore, wine tastings, and stargazing under the vast rural sky.

Multi-Purpose Room (SUM): A flexible space designed for yoga, wellness, corporate events, workshops, and exclusive private gatherings.
