The Complete Expedition

A detailed 16-day luxury journey from the "Paris of the South" to the 7th Continent.

Arrival at Buenos Aires International Airport, where you will be met and privately transferred to your hotel: Alvear Palace Hotel or Faena Hotel.

Evening: Welcome Dinner with fellow travelers.

A private city tour exploring iconic landmarks:

  • Plaza de Mayo & Teatro Colón
  • Leafy avenues of Palermo
  • Elegant Recoleta district
Evening: Premium Tango Dinner Show.

Fly south to the "End of the World." Check in at Los Cauquenes or Arakur.

Day 4: Full-day excursion to Tierra del Fuego National Park, featuring a scenic ride aboard the historic Tren del Fin del Mundo.

Morning luggage transfer. At 4:00 PM, board your Antarctic expedition ship. Meet your Expedition Team and enjoy your first welcome dinner onboard.

Cross the meeting point of the Atlantic and Pacific. Prepare for the white continent with polar expert presentations.

"Cross the Antarctic Convergence at 60° South where wildlife becomes abundant."

Arrive at a world of glaciers and dramatic ice formations. Daily Zodiac excursions exploring remote landing sites.

Wildlife: Whales, Orcas, Leopard Seals
Birds: Chinstrap, Adélie, & Gentoo Penguins

Day 12-13: Return crossing of the Drake. Celebrate the voyage with a final captain’s dinner.

Day 14-15: Disembark in Ushuaia, fly to Buenos Aires for a final overnight stay.

Day 16: Private transfer to Buenos Aires International Airport for your flight home.
