Expedition Itinerary

A curated 15-day journey from Buenos Aires to the South Pole.

1

Buenos Aires: The Paris of the South

Arrive at the international airport for a private transfer to the legendary Hotel Alvear Palace or Hotel Faena. Enjoy a private city tour followed by a premium Tango dinner show.

3

Ushuaia: The End of the World

Fly south to Ushuaia. Relax at Los Cauquenes or Arakur before exploring Tierra del Fuego National Park. On Day 5, we board our vessel at 4 PM.

6

The Drake Passage

Experience the legendary crossing. Attend expert presentations from our Expedition Team to prepare for land excursions as we cross 60°S.

8

Antarctic Peninsula & Shetland Islands

The heart of the expedition. Daily Zodiac excursions to glaciers and research stations. Witness gentoo penguins, humpback whales, and calving glaciers.

11

Reflection & Return

Recross the Drake, disembark in Ushuaia for a final overnight, then fly back to Buenos Aires for your international departure.
