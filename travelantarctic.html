Expedition Itinerary

A 16-day journey of a lifetime. Follow our route from the vibrant streets of Buenos Aires to the pristine wilderness of the Antarctic Peninsula.

Route: Buenos Aires — Ushuaia — Antarctic Peninsula

Arrival at Buenos Aires International Airport. Private transfer to your hotel: Alvear Palace Hotel or Faena Hotel.

Evening: Welcome Dinner to meet fellow travelers.

Full day private city tour of iconic neighborhoods:

  • Plaza de Mayo & Teatro Colón opera house
  • Leafy avenues of Palermo and elegant Recoleta
Evening: Premium Tango Dinner Show.

Flight to Ushuaia, the southernmost city in the world. Private transfer and check-in at Los Cauquenes Resort or Arakur Ushuaia with views of the Beagle Channel.

Full-day excursion exploring dramatic forests, mountains, and coastline. Includes a ride on the historic Tren del Fin del Mundo (Train at the End of the World).

Afternoon boarding around 4:00 PM. Welcome briefing, safety orientation, and your first gourmet dinner onboard as the expedition officially begins.

Cross the meeting point of the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Enjoy the observation lounge, seabird watching, and expert polar presentations.

"Officially enter Antarctic waters at the Antarctic Convergence (~60° South)."

Arrival at the 7th continent. Towers of ice and calving glaciers. Daily Zodiac excursions to remote sites.

Wildlife: Whales, Orcas, Leopard Seals
Penguins: Chinstrap, Adélie, Gentoo

Begin the journey north across the Drake Passage. Final onboard lectures and farewell celebrations reflecting on the expedition's impact.

  • Day 14: Disembark in Ushuaia (7:00 AM). Transfer to hotel for final overnight.
  • Day 15: Flight back to Buenos Aires. Private transfer to hotel.
  • Day 16: Private transfer to International Airport for your journey home.

Known as the "Resort at the End of the World," it is perched on a natural balcony 800 feet above sea level.
