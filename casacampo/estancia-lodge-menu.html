A Taste of the Argentine Countryside

Rooted in the traditions of Entre Ríos, our kitchen celebrates the honest flavors of the Argentine countryside.

From handmade empanadas and slow-cooked regional specialties to meats prepared over open fire, each dish reflects a connection to the land, its ingredients, and the traditions of gathering around the table.

A cuisine of generous portions, honest ingredients, and timeless hospitality.
