Arrive effortlessly. A seamless transition from the sky to the tranquility of the countryside.

The Fly-In Lodge Experience

Very few establishments in South America offer the true "Fly-In Lodge" experience. Our property features a private, highly-maintained aerodrome seamlessly integrated into the landscape, transforming your journey into the first highlight of your stay.

The airstrip is strategically positioned to provide maximum convenience upon landing, while ensuring total acoustic privacy for guests relaxing at the main lodge.

  • Runway Specs: 800 x 20 meters, perfectly suited for light aviation and turboprops (Cessna 182, 206, Piper, Beechcraft, Caravan C208).
  • Safety First: Features lateral safety strips, cleared thresholds, and an obstacle-free zone across 13.2 hectares.
  • VIP Reception: Disembark just meters from the hotel and be greeted instantly with a safari-style vehicle and a champagne welcome.

Hangar & Facilities

Beyond the runway, our infrastructure is designed to cater to the specific needs of pilots and aircraft owners. The on-site platform provides secure parking and serves as a hub for exclusive aeronautical events.

  • Private Hangar for secure storage.
  • Dedicated Pilot Lounge and operational office.
  • Basic maintenance facilities and meteorological updates.
  • Platform ideal for private aviation meets and scenic tourist flights.
