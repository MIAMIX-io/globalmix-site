The Experience

Designed for Serenity

Our Bespoke accommodation is intentionally scaled to maintain an atmosphere of profound tranquility. With a maximum capacity of just 22 guests across 10 exclusive suites, the lodge promises uncompromised privacy and deeply personalized attention.

The architecture ensures superior acoustic isolation, and every suite features its own exterior deck offering panoramic views directly from your bed.
