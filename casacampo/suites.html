Minimalist Luxury

Your private retreat blending modern comforts with the untamed beauty of the rural landscape.

Designed for Serenity

Our boutique accommodation is intentionally scaled to maintain an atmosphere of profound tranquility. With a maximum capacity of just 22 guests, the lodge promises uncompromised privacy and deeply personalized service.

The layout features an efficient central corridor separating the spaces, ensuring superior acoustic isolation. Every suite faces outward, offering uninterrupted, panoramic views of the horizon directly from your bed.

  • 8 Double Suites & 2 Triple Suites: Thoughtfully appointed for couples, executives, or small families.
  • Private Galleries: Each room features its own exterior deck, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor connection.
  • Natural Materials: Designed using local stone, warm woods, and premium textiles.

Uncompromising Comfort

Despite the remote setting, no luxury is spared. The architecture itself works to enhance your comfort, utilizing single-story design for ease of accessibility and high-end thermal efficiency to adapt to the Argentine seasons.

  • Radiant Heating: Ensuring perfect warmth during cool rural nights.
  • Dark-Sky Compliant: Low internal light pollution design to keep the focus on the stargazing experience outside.
  • Silenced Environment: Surrounded by native vegetation acting as natural wind and sound barriers.
