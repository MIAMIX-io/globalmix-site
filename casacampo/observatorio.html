The Destination

Where the countryside
and the cosmos meet

Observatorio Federal is an exclusive rural astrotourism destination nestled in the vast, unspoiled horizons of northern Entre Ríos. Benefiting from exceptionally low light pollution, we transform premium rural hospitality into an immersive scientific and sensory journey beneath the stars.

Our contemporary rural architecture integrates seamlessly into the nocturnal landscape through charcoal structures, warm stone, and reclaimed timber — an aesthetic designed not to compete with nature, but to honor the night sky.

Equipment & Facilities

A Rural Observatory
Under Open Skies

Equipped with professional-grade instruments and designed for both scientific observation and sensory wonder, the observatory is the technical soul of the estate.

🔭

Automated Telescopes

Professional-grade auto-tracking telescopes aligned for guided observation of planets, nebulae, and galaxies.

🌌

Immersive Dome

An astronomical dome for immersive projections, educational sessions, and introductory cosmic talks.

📷

Astrophotography Zone

Dedicated dark-sky platforms with stable mount points for long-exposure photography of the Milky Way.

🔴

Red-Light Systems

All pathways and observation areas use dim red lighting to preserve night-adapted vision.

🛋️

Reclining Deck

A terrace of reclining loungers angled toward the zenith — lie back and let the sky open above you.

🎓

Educational Programs

Structured programs for schools and universities with tailored workshops and observing sessions.

🌠

Astro Binoculars

High-power mounted binoculars for wide-field constellation tours — ideal for first-time sky watchers.

🏅

Dark Sky Path

On track for international Dark Sky Destination certification, protecting the night for generations.
