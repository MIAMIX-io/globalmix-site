Polo & Riding

The Argentine Tradition

Casa Campo boasts world-class equestrian facilities that honor the deeply rooted horse culture of Argentina. Whether you are an experienced rider or a curious beginner, the expansive fields of our estate provide the perfect terrain.

Mount up to explore hidden trails, ride alongside our expert gauchos, or learn the "Sport of Kings" on our private polo fields.

Polo Lessons

Introductory and advanced polo clinics available upon request with professional instructors.

Trail Riding

Guided horseback expeditions through native forests and the expansive open pampas.

Wilderness Excursions

Hunting & Fishing

Northern Entre Ríos is world-renowned for its diverse ecology and exceptional wildlife reserves. Casa Campo organizes exclusive, highly regulated outdoor excursions guided by local experts.

Golden Dorado Fishing

Chartered trips to the nearby Paraná River system, offering world-class catch-and-release fishing for the legendary Golden Dorado.

Guided Hunting

Access exclusive local reserves for seasonal hunting excursions. Fully equipped and guided for absolute safety and compliance.
