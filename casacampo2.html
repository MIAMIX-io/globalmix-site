The Estate

Where the countryside
and the cosmos meet

Observatorio Federal is a premier rural astrotourism destination nestled in the vast, unspoiled horizons of northern Entre Ríos. Here, minimal light pollution and boundless rural skies create extraordinary conditions for experiencing the night sky as nature intended — immense, alive, and deeply humbling.

Our contemporary rural architecture integrates seamlessly into the nocturnal landscape through charcoal structures, warm stone, and reclaimed timber — an aesthetic designed not to compete with nature, but to honor it.

Astronomical Infrastructure

A Rural Observatory
Under Open Skies

Equipped with professional-grade instruments and designed for both scientific observation and sensory wonder, the observatory is the soul of Observatorio Federal.

🔭

Automated Telescopes

Professional-grade auto-tracking telescopes aligned for guided observation of stars, planets, nebulae, and galaxies throughout the year.

🌌

Immersive Dome

An astronomical dome for immersive projections, educational sessions, and introductory astronomical talks in any weather condition.

📷

Astrophotography Zone

Dedicated dark-sky platforms with stable mount points for long-exposure photography of the Milky Way, star trails, and planetary surfaces.

🔴

Red-Light Systems

All pathways and observation areas use dim red lighting to preserve night-adapted vision — a detail that transforms the entire experience.

🛋️

Reclining Observation Deck

A terrace of reclining loungers angled toward the zenith — lie back and let the sky open above you in panoramic darkness.

🎓

Educational Programs

Structured programs for schools, universities, and science communities with tailored talks, workshops, and hands-on observing sessions.

🌠

Astronomical Binoculars

High-power mounted binoculars for wide-field constellation tours — ideal for first-time visitors discovering the sky for the first time.

🏅

Dark Sky Certification Path

On track for international Dark Sky Destination certification — positioning Observatorio Federal among the world's premier astrotourism sites.
