A Dark Sky Destination

OBSERVATORIO FEDERAL is an exclusive rural astrotourism destination designed to seamlessly integrate into the natural landscape. Benefiting from exceptionally low light pollution, we transform premium rural hospitality into an immersive scientific and sensory journey beneath the stars.

Private Fly-In Access

Arrive effortlessly. Our property features a private, illuminated 20-meter wide airstrip, allowing exclusive aircraft access directly to the resort grounds.

Regional Gastronomy

Savor authentic flavors in our panoramic dining room. Featuring an open-concept kitchen, warm wood beams, and expansive glass walls that merge the indoors with the rural horizon.

Minimalist Luxury

Retreat to elegance. Our suites utilize stone, wood, and a dark visual palette to minimize light pollution, ensuring your focus remains on the breathtaking landscape.

Contemporary Rural Architecture

Our aesthetic language is built on charcoal black, smoke gray, and deep night blue. The architecture uses horizontal designs that respect the landscape, providing views from every room.

  • 10 Exclusive Suites: Designed for maximum comfort with radiant heating and natural materials.
  • Immersive Observatory: Equipped with automatic telescopes and red-light systems for perfect night vision adaptation.
  • Harmonic Design: Predominant dark metallic and stone details reduce internal light pollution, turning the hotel into a visual extension of the night.
