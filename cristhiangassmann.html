About

From Entre Ríos to the world

Raised between Entre Ríos and Buenos Aires, Cristhian has lived and traveled across the globe before making his home in the United States — first in New York, then Miami. His career spans aviation, travel planning, tour guiding, hospitality and television, and it all comes together in one mission: connecting people with unforgettable places.

Journey

A career in motion

Cristhian Gassmann is an international television host, journalist, media entrepreneur, and travel industry professional with more than 20 years of experience across media, tourism, lifestyle, and business development.

His journey began in travel and tourism, where he built a deep understanding of hospitality, destinations, and memorable guest experiences. That foundation grew into international media, combining his knowledge of destinations with his ability to communicate, interview, produce, and connect with audiences.

Based in Miami, Florida, he develops media projects, strategic partnerships, and international collaborations through MIAMIX.
